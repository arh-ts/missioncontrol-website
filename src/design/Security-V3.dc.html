Security and privacy

Designed to pass your security review.

Mission Control maps how work moves. It is built to capture as little as possible, to be rolled out openly, and to stand up to scrutiny from your IT, legal and people teams.

Request documentation →

WHAT WE CAPTURE

The movement of work.

  • When work moves between people, teams and systems
  • How long each step takes
  • Status changes and hand-offs
  • Which system a piece of work moved through

WHAT WE NEVER CAPTURE

What the work says.

  • The contents of emails or messages
  • The contents of files and documents
  • Client or matter details inside your systems
  • Rankings of individual staff

Rolling out with your people

Open by default. No surprises for staff.

Mission Control measures how work moves, not how individuals perform. We help you say that clearly, before anything is switched on.

  1. 01
    Brief leadership
    Agree scope, purpose and what will be said to staff.
  2. 02
    Notify staff in writing
    Plain-language notice of what is captured and why, ahead of switch-on, in line with workplace surveillance rules where you operate.
  3. 03
    Answer questions
    A short session for teams, with our team present.
  4. 04
    Switch on and review
    Start with the agreed scope and review it together.
