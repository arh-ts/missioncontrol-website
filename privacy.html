Privacy notice

How we handle personal information on this website and in client deployments.

Last updated 22 November 2026

About this notice

This notice explains how Mission Control ("we", "us") handles personal information when you visit missioncntrl.io, contact us or book a briefing. It also explains, at a high level, how personal information is handled when we deploy Mission Control for a client organisation. We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Where they apply, we also follow the UK GDPR and the privacy laws of US states such as California.

What we collect through this website

Information you give us

When you book a briefing or email us we collect what you choose to provide: your name, work email, organisation, role, organisation size, industry and any message you write.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs when pages are requested, including IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. We use these logs to keep the site secure and working.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or third-party tracking pixels. Fonts, images and videos are served from our own domain, so loading a page does not send your details to font or video providers. We store one small value in your browser's session storage so the opening animation plays only once per visit. It contains no personal information and is cleared when you close the tab.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange and prepare for a briefing.
  • To follow up about Mission Control if you have asked us to, or where it is reasonable to expect we will. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet legal obligations.

For visitors in the UK, our lawful bases are our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and running our website, and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

When we deploy Mission Control for a client

When an organisation engages us, Mission Control processes information about how work moves through that organisation's systems: when work moves between people, teams and systems, how long steps take, and status changes and hand-offs. By design it does not capture the contents of emails, messages, files or documents, client or matter details inside those systems, or rankings of individual staff.

In a client deployment the client organisation decides the scope and purpose of processing, and we act on its instructions under our agreement with it. The client is responsible for notifying its people and for meeting workplace surveillance and privacy obligations where it operates, and we support it to do so. If you work for one of our clients and have a question about how your information is handled, please contact your employer first. You can also contact us.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as website hosting, email delivery and email hosting providers, and only for that purpose. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. Where we disclose personal information overseas we take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected in a way that is consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and any resulting relationship, and then delete or de-identify it unless we must keep it longer by law. Server logs are kept for a short period by our hosting provider.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Access to enquiry information is limited to the people who need it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to ask us to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy in a portable form. Contact us at hello@missioncntrl.io. We will respond within a reasonable time, and within any period required by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Complaints

If you are concerned about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. In the UK you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Email hello@missioncntrl.io with "Privacy" in the subject line.
