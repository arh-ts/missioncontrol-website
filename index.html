Why it matters

The org chart shows who reports to whom. It doesn't show how work gets done.

Hand-offs are invisible

Work moves between people, inboxes and systems all day. Nobody sees the whole route.

Delays hide in the gaps

A file waits two days for one approver. It shows up as a missed deadline, not a cause.

Automation budgets are a guess

Money gets spent on what is loudest, not on what returns the most time.
